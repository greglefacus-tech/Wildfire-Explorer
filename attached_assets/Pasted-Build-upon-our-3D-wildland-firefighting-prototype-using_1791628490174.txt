Build upon our 3D wildland firefighting prototype using Three.js. We want to add realistic fire behavior, real-life fuel dynamics, wind controls, and a firefighter trenching tool.

Please implement the following features:

1. Dynamic Fire System (Cellular Automata Engine):
   - Grid-Based Spread: Overlay a logical fuel grid on top of the terrain map.
   - Fire Scale & Intensity: 
     * Small, slow-moving ground fires in light grass that are easy to contain.
     * Massive, intense crown fires taller than trees when igniting dense trees/bushes under high wind.
   - Realistic Fuel Dynamics:
     * Grass: Burns quickly with low flame height, acts as fast-moving surface fuel.
     * Trees & Bushes: Take longer to ignite, but burn hotter and longer with huge flames and dark smoke.
     * Dirt / Firebreaks: Have 0 fuel and permanently block fire propagation.

2. Map Border Firefront & Ignition:
   - Turn the outer edge/border of the map into the main advancing firefront.
   - The fire line along the edge slowly creeps inward into the forest, with its speed and direction heavily driven by wind.

3. Wind Variables & Real-Time HUD Controls:
   - Expose real-time UI controls (sliders) for:
     * Wind Direction (0° to 360°)
     * Wind Speed (Light Breeze to High Winds)
   - Fire spread probability and flame particle direction must dynamically tilt and push downwind according to these sliders.

4. Firefighter Digging Tool (Pulaski / Trench Line):
   - When the firefighter holds Left Click on the ground, scrape away grass and vegetation to create a dirt firebreak trench.
   - Visual Feedback: Change the terrain surface color to bare dirt brown where scraped, and immediately clear the fuel value of those cells to stop advancing flames.

5. Controls & Cursor Unlock:
   - WASD to walk, Mouse to look around.
   - Pressing the 'P' key cleanly toggles the mouse pointer lock on and off (unlocking the cursor lets the user click UI buttons and adjust sliders without opening the pause menu).
   - Pressing 'Esc' or 'Tab' opens the main options menu.

Make the visual fire particle effects (sparks, rising smoke, glowing embers) look vibrant and high-contrast, keeping the code clean, performant, and self-contained in index.html.
